# Precedentes privados, fintech y open source de índices de inflación personal o alternativa

Research date: 2026-10-26. Method: web search and fetches of primary pages (company blogs, GitHub repo pages, GitHub web search, HN Algolia API). The GitHub REST search API was blocked in this session (403, "sessions are bound to their configured repositories"), so the GitHub inventory comes from github.com/search pages fetched with WebFetch, which were rate-limited (HTTP 429) part of the time. Star counts and "last updated" values are what GitHub showed on 2026-10-26. Relative dates are converted to approximate absolute dates, and dates shown without a year are 2026. "Last updated" on GitHub search means the last push, which is not necessarily a meaningful commit.

## 1. Fintech and bank features: has any bank or PFM app offered "your personal inflation" computed from transactions?

### Takeaway
No mainstream bank or personal-finance app turned up with an automated "your inflation rate" feature built from linked transactions. That covers every app on the list: Monzo, Revolut, N26, Starling, Lloyds, Klarna, Nubank, BBVA, Santander, Mercado Pago, Nu México, Fintonic, Emma, Copilot, Monarch, YNAB, Mint, Spendee, BudgetBakers, Finerio, Albo, Klar and Stori. What exists falls into three groups: (a) manual calculators where the user types in category spending, run by statistics offices, media, think tanks and Truflation; (b) educational blog posts from apps such as Emma, BBVA and Klar telling users to work it out themselves; and (c) a handful of tiny indie apps (ExpensePal, Pocket Index, thrust) whose traction is minimal or that don't actually automate it.

### Cited Findings
**Banks and PFM apps (no automated feature found)**
- **N26** has an "inflation calculator", but it is a generic purchasing-power tool. The user types an amount, an inflation % (capped at 30%) and a number of years. It does not use N26 transactions. — [N26](https://n26.com/en-eu/inflation-calculator)
- **Emma (UK PFM)**: its blog post "How to calculate your personal inflation rate" (5 Mar 2021, metadata updated 14 Mar 2025) gives only manual guidance: divide this year's spending by last year's (e.g., £21K/£20K = 5%). It describes no in-app feature using linked transactions. — [Emma blog](https://emma-app.com/blog/2021/03/05/how-to-calculate-your-personal-inflation-rate)
- **BBVA México (Bancomer)**: the article "La inflación personal" (updated 25 Jan 2017, by Viart Vázquez Smith, VP of Banca Patrimonial y Privada, originally a column in El Economista) explains why personal inflation differs from the INPC, which then had 283 conceptos. It offers no tool or app feature. — [BBVA](https://www.bbva.com/es/la-inflacion-personal/)
- **Mercado Pago México** markets auto-categorizing some transactions and exporting the movement history (company marketing). It has no inflation feature. — [Mercado Pago blog](https://www.mercadopago.com.mx/blog/finanzas-personales)
- **Nu México and Klar**: a third-party review describes Nu's "Cajitas" and its "Salud Financiera" spending analysis, and Klar's budgeting and "Apartados". Neither has an inflation feature. Stori and Albo didn't appear in the results at all. — [financera.mx review](https://financera.mx/finanzas-personales/aplicaciones-finanzas-personales/)
- **Klar** publishes an educational post, "¿Cómo calcular la inflación?" (education, not a product feature). — [Klar](https://www.klar.mx/post/como-calcular-la-inflacion)
- **Snoop (UK)**: according to a review, its AI flags "price hikes on regular bills" and cheaper alternatives. That is bill-level monitoring, not a personal inflation rate. — [search summary of UK app reviews](https://medium.com/@mauriciotorob/the-best-spend-trackers-in-the-uk-030d6d9d13e2)
- **Fintonic (Spain)** aggregates BBVA, Santander, Sabadell, CaixaBank, Openbank and others. No "IPC personal" feature was found. — [Fintonic help](https://fintonic.ladesk.com/261786-Qu%C3%A9-bancos-puedo-conectar-en-Fintonic); [Genbeta](https://www.genbeta.com/a-fondo/gestionar-todos-tus-bancos-unico-sitio-11-apps-banca-que-permiten-controlar-todos-tus-gastos)
- **ABN AMRO, ING, Rabobank, bunq (NL)**: bank apps with categorized spending and multi-bank aggregation. **Mexican aggregator relevant as a data pipe (not an inflation product)**
- **Finerio** was founded in 2016 in Mexico City as a PFM where users aggregate bank accounts and track spending. From 2020 it opened its infrastructure to banks, neobanks, lenders and others, and moved into open banking with **Finerio Connect**, an API for accounts, balances and transactions. It had alliances with Bancolombia (2022), BancoEstado Chile (2021) and Guiabolso. — [LatamFintech](https://www.latamfintech.co/articles/pfm-de-mexico-finerio-connect-aterriza-en-colombia-en-alianza-con-bancolombia-para-hacer-open-banking); [El Dínamo](https://www.eldinamo.cl/empren-d/2021/10/27/bancoestado-y-startup-fintech-firman-acuerdo-para-impulsar-openbanking/)

- **ExpensePal** is an Android expense tracker with a "Personal Inflation Score" whose method is unexplained. On Product Hunt it got 8 upvotes, 1 comment and ranked #58 for the day. It is built by a solo founder in India. — [hunted.space](https://www.hunted.space/product/expensepal)
- **thrust** is an EU "privacy-first money assistant" for iPhone, still in preview. Its June 20, 2026 article explains the category-weighted personal inflation method, but accounts are added manually or by CSV, and the app doesn't compute the rate automatically. It only shows 12-month category spending next to the previous 12 months. — [thrust](https://thrust.finance/learn/personal-inflation-rate/)
- **Simplsaver (Australia)**: a budgeting app reported to be launching a tool that weighs a user's budget against CPI. It is a budget-stress tool, not a personal index. — [savings.com.au](https://savings.com.au/news/simplsaver-app-review)
- **Finhabits** (a US fintech) has a "Personal Inflation: Calculate Your Real Cost of Living" content page (content marketing; the feature was not verified). — [Finhabits](https://www.finhabits.com/personal-inflation/)
- **Infleta (Argentina, 2021)**, built by Ariel Díaz, helps users decide between paying cash or in installments under high inflation. It is not a personal index. — [Unidiversidad](https://www.unidiversidad.com.ar/infleta-la-app-para-cuidar-los-pesos-y-calcular-mejor-la-forma-de-pago26)
- **Richify** is an AI personal-finance assistant with a free 7-day trial and a mobile app. Its "Termómetro Anti-Inflación" takes salary, raise and years in the job, but for Mexico it uses the generic INPC ratio, not a personal basket. It covers 9 countries and was supported by the ElevenLabs Startup Grants. — [Richify](https://www.richify.ai/es/termometro-inflacion)

- **Media "methods"** are simple: compare one month's total spending year over year (CNBC, May 2022). **Bank card data used for spending (not inflation) analytics**
- The Bank of America Institute "Consumer Checkpoint" tracks card spending per household by income and generation, e.g., +2.0% YoY in September 2025. This measures spending growth, not a price index. — [BofA Institute](https://institute.bankofamerica.com/content/dam/economic-insights/consumer-checkpoint-october-2025.pdf)
- A 2026 arXiv paper warns that card data from a single bank, fintech or processor may not represent the population and should be validated against benchmarks. — [arXiv 2607.08759](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.08759)
- Truflation's older US methodology lists Plaid and credit card data among its sources, and it derives US weights for 2026 from credit card transaction data, Census data and expenditure surveys. This is aggregate use of transaction data to set CPI weights, not per-user. — [Truflation 2026 weights report](https://truflation.com/blog/truflation-2026-cpi-weights-report); [Truflation methodology PDF](https://truflation.com/Methodology.pdf)

### Inferences
- The specific combination the user wants (linked bank transactions, mapped to official CPI sub-indices, a monthly personal index) seems to have no notable commercial precedent in Mexico or LatAm, and none found globally among major banks or PFMs. The space looks open, but the lack of precedent is also a warning about demand and monetization: the PFM players with the data (Emma, Fintonic, Nu, Mercado Pago) wrote blog posts instead of building the feature.
- Finerio Connect, or another Mexican aggregator, is the obvious "bank connection" layer for a Mexican product. The Mercado Pago movement export is a no-integration fallback.
- Demand spikes with inflation episodes. ONS launched its calculator during the 2022 surge, INDEC in Dec 2024, and Truflation's calculators came in 2023–2024. A Mexican product launched when inflation is around 3.3% should expect a weaker novelty spike.

### Gaps
- For Monzo, Revolut, Starling, Lloyds, Klarna, Nubank, Santander, Copilot, Monarch, YNAB, Mint, Spendee, Wallet by BudgetBakers, Fudo, Albo and Stori, searches found **no evidence** of a personal-inflation feature, but the search tool doesn't index app changelogs, help centers or forums well. Treat this as "not found", not "confirmed absent".
- Mint's shutdown and its date were not verified in this session.
- No usage numbers were found for INDEC's, StatCan's, ECB's or FGV's calculators. ONS's "1 million" is the only traction figure found.
- The FGV portal page was unreachable (503), so its details come from the search summary only.

## 2. Alternative and private inflation indices: Truflation, PriceStats/BPP, Chapwood, ShadowStats, Numbeo, Argentina, Turkey, Mexico private efforts and media

### Takeaway
The private indices that have survived are **B2B data businesses**. PriceStats (online-price scraping) was acquired by State Street in Nov 2025. Truflation is now a crypto/data network funded by a token and API access, covering only the US, UK, Argentina and India, with **no Mexico index**. Consumer-facing alternatives built on distrust (ShadowStats, Chapwood) are widely discredited methodologically. In LatAm, alternative indices became politically dangerous where official statistics were contested (Argentina 2011 fines, Turkey 2022 lawsuits). In Mexico, nobody was found running a private personal or alternative CPI. The closest things are Profeco's government price survey, generic INPC calculators, op-eds proposing to "undress" the INPC into its genéricos, and a hobby "Índice Gansito" repo.

### Cited Findings
**Truflation**
- Founded in 2021 and based in Austin, Texas. — [search summary citing CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/truflation)
- Its documents page (fetched 2026-10-26) lists country CPIs for **US (TruCPI-US, methodology v1.4, 2026-03-26), UK (TruCPI-UK v1.4, 2026-05-06) and Argentina (TruCPI-AR v1.1, 2026-05-14)**, plus many thematic indexes (PCE, Eggs, Gas, Housing, Oil, EV, "TruCPIWarsh-US" Jun 2026, etc.). **There is no Mexico index or document.** — [Truflation documents](https://truflation.com/documents)
- India: Truflation launched an India CPI and personal calculator, but India doesn't appear on the 2026 documents page, a discrepancy worth noting. — [Truflation blog India](https://blog.truflation.com/truflation-launches-independent-inflation-tracker-for-india/); [2024 Year in Review](https://blog.truflation.com/2024-year-in-review-truflations-unprecedented-growth-and-global-impact/)
- Data: "30+ data sources and 15+ million prices" per country, daily (from the search summary of its methodology). The 2024 review claims 80+ sources and more than 30M data points on TRUF.Network. US 2026 providers include Zillow, AAA, Kantar, NielsenIQ, Insurify and Amazon, and US weights are updated annually. — [TruCPI-US methodology v1.4](https://ddsvqvocwuhvopwpzmgo.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/site-files/indexes/1774868904122-TruCPI-US%20Index%20-%202026%20-%20Methodology%20v1.4%20(2026-03-26).pdf); [2026 weights report](https://truflation.com/blog/truflation-2026-cpi-weights-report); [2024 review](https://blog.truflation.com/2024-year-in-review-truflations-unprecedented-growth-and-global-impact/)
- It self-reports an average deviation of 0.1% from BLS CPI. This is a company claim and wasn't independently verified. — [2024 review](https://blog.truflation.com/2024-year-in-review-truflations-unprecedented-growth-and-global-impact/)
- **Business model**: TRUF is an ERC-20 token. Data providers stake it to run nodes, and users pay it to access data. — [whitepaper](https://whitepaper.truflation.com/truf-token/overview)
- Truflation was folded into **TRUF.NETWORK**, an umbrella over Truflation, Nuon.fi and Index.fun. — [Truflation blog](https://blog.truflation.com/truflation-truf-network-and-the-truf-token-the-next-chapter/)
- The token vests over 96 months, with 9.4% of supply circulating at launch. — [TRUF token post](https://blog.truflation.com/truf-token-powering-permissionless-access-to-financial-data/)
- TRUF is listed on KuCoin, Bitstamp, MEXC and Gate.io. Named DeFi partners include Synthetix, dYdX, Overlay, Perennial, Copper.co and others. — [2024 review](https://blog.truflation.com/2024-year-in-review-truflations-unprecedented-growth-and-global-impact/)
- Dealroom *estimates* revenue comes mainly from API and data-feed subscriptions. This is not a company disclosure. — [Dealroom](https://dealroom.co/companies/truflation/)
- **Personal calculator**: inputs are manual (salary plus expenses, or a preset persona such as "US urban professional" or "US retired senior"). It shows the "salary cut" from inflation and the salary needed to keep the same standard of living (US post, 21 Dec 2023). No usage numbers have been published. — [Truflation blog](https://blog.truflation.com/try-the-truflation/)
- The company also markets the calculator for salary negotiation. — [Truflation blog "get a raise"](https://blog.truflation.com/how-to-use-truflations-personal-inflation-calculator-to-get-a-raise/)
- **Argentina (20 Aug 2024)**: an AR inflation index and personal calculator (salary, bills and expenses), "developed with the Argentine government" (the entity isn't named), announced in a sponsored Chainwire release with no user numbers. — [Decrypt/Chainwire](https://decrypt.co/245475/truflation-and-argentinian-government-launch-independent-inflation-tracker-and-calculator); [Truflation blog AR](https://blog.truflation.com/truflation-launches-independent-inflation-tracker-and-calculator-for-argentina/)

**PriceStats / State Street / Billion Prices Project (Cavallo, Rigobon)**
- Cavallo began building daily online-price inflation series for Latin American countries in 2007. Rigobon joined a year later and expanded the work to 50 countries. — [Central Banking](https://www.centralbanking.com/node/3346716)
- PriceStats was founded in 2011 and has been part of State Street's research platform since then. **State Street acquired PriceStats on 10 Nov 2025** (terms undisclosed). It now sits in State Street Data Intelligence, and Cavallo remains an academic partner. The firm covers millions of products from more than 1,500 retailers, and its clients are "institutional investors, economists, and central banks" (none named). — [Financial Investigator](https://www.financialinvestigator.nl/en/nieuws-detailpagina/2025/11/10/state-street-acquires-pricestats-global-leader-in-inflation-analytics)
- State Street's product page: daily country inflation indices and PPP indicators (matched basket of about 250 goods) across **27 countries**. The countries aren't named, so **Mexico coverage is unconfirmed**. — [State Street PriceStats](https://www.statestreet.com/us/en/solutions/data-intelligence/pricestats)
- **The Billion Prices Project (founded 2008) is "no longer active"**, but its datasets remain downloadable. **Inflación Verdadera** (Argentina, 2007) published food and basic-basket indices from daily prices at two big Buenos Aires supermarkets during 2007–2012, then switched to a PriceStats CPI in Aug 2012. It describes itself as the academic project that showed Argentina's real inflation in 2007–2015. — [thebillionpricesproject.com / Inflación Verdadera](https://thebillionpricesproject.com/)
- Cavallo's 2013 JME paper, "Online and Official Price Indexes: Measuring Argentina's Inflation", used supermarket data from Oct 2007 to Mar 2011. INDEC suspended its CPI in Dec 2015. — [Harvard CID](https://www.hks.harvard.edu/centers/cid/publications/online-and-official-price-indexes-measuring-argentinas-inflation); [FillingTheGap (Cavallo & Bertolotto)](https://www.thebillionpricesproject.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2025/09/FillingTheGap_es.pdf)

**Argentina: consultoras, political risk and bots**
- In March 2011 the Secretaría de Comercio Interior (Guillermo Moreno) fined private consultancies **ARS 500,000** under the Ley de Lealtad Comercial for publishing alternative price indices. M&S Consultores, Econviews, Estudio Bein, Ecolatina and Finsport were among those fined. — [Chequeado](https://chequeado.com/hilando-fino/a-falta-de-datos-de-inflacion/)
- To shield the consultancies, opposition legislators published the **"IPC Congreso"**, an average of their readings. The first release in June 2011 showed 1.5% m/m for May and 23.5% y/y. — [Chequeado](https://chequeado.com/hilando-fino/a-falta-de-datos-de-inflacion/); [Ámbito](https://www.ambito.com/edicion-impresa/el-indice-congreso-a-prueba-moreno-fue-del-15-mayo-n3687697)
- Chequeado says the Supreme Court dismissed the fines, while Moreno said the Court hadn't ruled, so the sources conflict. Judge Bonadío indicted Moreno in Sept 2013, and a trial followed. — [Chequeado](https://chequeado.com/hilando-fino/a-falta-de-datos-de-inflacion/); [La Nación](https://www.lanacion.com.ar/politica/arranca-juicio-guillermo-moreno-presionar-consultoras-privadas-nid2284213)
- Supermarket Twitter bots (Jumbot, Coto_bot) were fact-checked by Chequeado: Jumbot was a fake account and Coto_bot showed unofficial data. Bot data isn't a reliable indicator of general price movements. — [Chequeado](https://chequeado.com/el-explicador/bots-de-supermercados-e-inflacion-jumbot-es-una-cuenta-falsa-y-coto_botmuestra-datos-no-oficiales/)

**Turkey: ENAG (comparable political-risk case)**
- TÜİK sued ENAG (an independent academic group led by Veysel Ulusoy) in early 2022, demanding it stop operating and alleging it didn't publish explanatory methodology. The courts dismissed the case, and the Finance Ministry opened a tax audit of ENAG in 2022. — [Duvar English](https://www.duvarenglish.com/turkish-govt-statistics-office-tuik-files-lawsuit-against-enagroup-over-alternative-inflation-data-news-57435); [Bianet](https://bianet.org/english/law/269457-court-drops-case-against-inflation-research-group); [Duvar, tax audit](https://www.duvarenglish.com/turkish-finance-ministry-launches-tax-audit-against-independent-inflation-research-group-enag-news-61181)

**ShadowStats and Chapwood**
- **ShadowStats** (John Williams): critics say it is not a recomputation. It takes the official CPI and adds a fixed "fudge factor" for estimated understatement, and Williams himself described using government estimates as an add factor. — [Full Stack Economics](https://www.fullstackeconomics.com/p/no-the-real-inflation-rate-isnt-14-percent); [Econbrowser](https://econbrowser.com/?p=9388); [Noahpinion](https://www.noahpinion.blog/p/inflation-is-up-but-the-inflation)
- **Chapwood Index**: promotional write-ups describe 500 items in the 50 largest US cities, released twice a year. Critics cite a tiny sample and selection bias (2015). An April 2025 mailing-list post said the site still existed but no methodology could be found. — [cohousing-l (2025)](https://lists.cohousing.org/archives/cohousing-l/msg51334.html); [Full Stack Economics](https://www.fullstackeconomics.com/p/no-the-real-inflation-rate-isnt-14-percent)

**Numbeo**
- Mostly crowdsourced user submissions, plus manually collected data from supermarket, taxi and government sites entered twice a year and weighted 3x. More than 30 proprietary spam and outlier filters. Indices are relative to NYC = 100. Data older than 12 months is dropped by default (3 months for popular cities, up to 24 months when contributors are few). — [Numbeo methodology](https://www.numbeo.com/common/motivation_and_methodology.jsp)

**Mexico: private and media efforts**
- **Profeco "Quién es Quién en los Precios"** is government, not private, but it is the main public price-scraping-like source. It monitors more than 1,800 establishments in 32 cities across 30 states and has a 24-product PACIC basket that is reported weekly at the mañanera. — [ANTAD-hosted Profeco PDF](https://antad.net/admin/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/PROFECO.pdf); [Por Esto](https://www.poresto.com/mexico/2025/7/14/profeco-revela-donde-esta-la-canasta-basica-mas-barata-y-lanza-operativo-de-verano-contra-abusos.amp.html)
- **Expansión op-ed "La inflación al desnudo"** (Iván Franco, Triplethree International, 10 Mar 2021) proposes breaking the INPC into its 297 genéricos and looking at them unweighted. February 2021 headline inflation was 3.76% vs. a 4.28% simple average of genéricos, and 110 genéricos rose 5–10%. It offers no tool or dataset. — [Expansión](https://expansion.mx/opinion/2021/03/10/la-inflacion-al-desnudo)
- **El Financiero column (Marco Pérez, 19 Nov 2025)**: the INPC basket represents only average consumption of an average family. — [El Financiero](https://www.elfinanciero.com.mx/monterrey/2025/11/19/marco-perez-el-indice-nacional-de-precios-al-consumidor-mide-en-realidad-la-inflacion/)
- **Generic INPC calculators** abound (hazlacuenta.mx, vuallet.com, calculadorainpc.com, calcu.mx, cotizadorhipotecario.mx, calcforlife, contadormx, El Universal "consultas/inpc"). They all apply the ratio INPC_final / INPC_initial to an amount, and none offers a personal basket. Their annual figures sometimes disagree (one says 4.1% for 2025, another 3.8%). — [calcforlife](https://calcforlife.com/?p=2456); [calculoseguro.mx](https://calculoseguro.mx/calculadora-inflacion-mexico/); [cotizadorhipotecario.mx](https://cotizadorhipotecario.mx/ahorro/calculadora-inflacion); [hazlacuenta.mx](https://hazlacuenta.mx/calculadoras/inflacion); [El Universal](https://www.eluniversal.com.mx/consultas/inpc/)
- **IIEG Jalisco** (a state government institute) publishes a technical note on monitoring Jalisco inflation by product or service. That shows genérico-level series are being repackaged locally. — [IIEG nota técnica](https://iieg.gob.mx/ns/wp-content/uploads/2022/01/Nota-t%C3%A9cnica-Monitoreo-de-la-inflaci%C3%B3n-en-Jalisco-por-producto-o-servicio.pdf)
- **"Índice Gansito"** (GitHub AldoMor00/indice-gansito-datos, 0 stars, updated ~2026-10-22) is a raw data store combining Profeco prices, the minimum wage and INPC from automated ingestion. ### Inferences
- Truflation has no Mexico index, and its personal calculator is manual and persona-based, so it isn't a direct competitor in Mexico. It does validate the "salary-cut / raise negotiation" framing, and it shows that consumer calculators serve mainly as marketing funnels for a B2B or crypto data business.
- PriceStats and Truflation solve a *different* problem from the user's project: producing an alternative *price* measurement. The user's project reweights *official* INEGI genérico indices by personal *weights*. That is methodologically lighter and politically low-risk, because it doesn't challenge INEGI's prices. It avoids the Argentina and Turkey failure mode, where alternative price readings drew fines and lawsuits.
- ShadowStats and Chapwood show the reputational cost of opaque methods. A transparent, reproducible method (a published Laspeyres formula, open code, reconciliation against INEGI headline) is a differentiator.

## 5. Cross-cutting lessons: why personal-inflation tools struggle, what traction exists, what made survivors stick

### Takeaway
Personal-inflation tools get **episodic, inflation-driven attention** but little sustained use. The only hard traction figure found is ONS's ~1M uses in 2022. Indie and open-source tools have single-digit Product Hunt upvotes and GitHub stars. The survivors either sell data B2B (PriceStats, now part of State Street; Truflation through token and API), are public goods run by statistics offices, or are free calculators used as marketing funnels. A key methodological limit: academic evidence shows **most household-level inflation dispersion comes from differences in prices paid for the same goods, not from basket weights**, so CPI reweighting from bank data captures only part of a person's "real" inflation.

### Cited Findings
- **Traction signals**:
  - ONS calculator: more than 1M uses or people in 2022. — [ONS](https://blog.ons.gov.uk/2022/12/23/the-year-in-numbers/)
  - ExpensePal: 8 upvotes, #58 on Product Hunt. — [hunted.space](https://www.hunted.space/product/expensepal)
  - HN "personal inflation" posts: at most 2 points. yourcpi.com in 2009 got 1 point. — [HN 762130](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=762130)
  - Top GitHub personal-inflation repo: 13★, dormant since 2023. — [GitHub](https://github.com/n8ngo/puffer-personal-inflation-index)
  - Truflation publishes no calculator usage figures. — [Truflation blog](https://blog.truflation.com/try-the-truflation/)
- **Willingness-to-engage signal**: HN's "How to ask employer to match inflation rate" (Apr 2022, 55 points, 101 comments). Truflation also markets its calculator for negotiating raises. Together these suggest the **wage-negotiation and "salary cut" use case** resonates more than abstract measurement. — [HN 31166006](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=31166006); [Truflation](https://blog.truflation.com/how-to-use-truflations-personal-inflation-calculator-to-get-a-raise/)
- **Methodological limits**:
  - Kaplan & Schulhofer-Wohl (scanner data) find household inflation rates vary widely. **Most of the variation comes from households paying different prices for the same goods, not from different consumption bundles.** Lower-income households see higher inflation, but most variation is uncorrelated with observables. — [NBER w22331](https://www.nber.org/system/files/working_papers/w22331/w22331.pdf); [JME](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0304393217300879)
  - With income-stratified PCE indices, gaps across income groups are much smaller than CPI-based estimates. — [BEA WP2025-9](https://www.bea.gov/system/files/papers/BEA-WP2025-9.pdf)
  - Transaction-based indices are prone to chain drift. With heterogeneous preferences, an aggregate transaction index may not match any individual's cost of living. — [JBES 2025](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/07350015.2025.2537392)
  - Jaravel's distributional CPIs, and the MPRA household-basket study (poorer households lose more purchasing power), support heterogeneity in inflation by income. — [Jaravel](https://events.berkeley.edu/live/files/26-dcpipdf); [MPRA 105461](https://mpra.ub.uni-muenchen.de/105461/1/MPRA_paper_105461.pdf)
- **"Year-over-year total spending" is not inflation**: the popular media method (CNBC, NBC, Emma) compares total spending across years. That mixes price changes with quantity and lifestyle changes, a common conceptual flaw in consumer tools. — [NBC](https://www.nbcnews.com/business/consumer/calculate-personal-inflation-rate-means-inflation-rate-calculate-rcna28442); [Emma](https://emma-app.com/blog/2021/03/05/how-to-calculate-your-personal-inflation-rate)
- **Political and legal risk for alternative *price* indices in LatAm and similar settings**: Argentina's 2011 ARS 500k fines, the Moreno prosecution, and Turkey's 2022 TÜİK lawsuits and tax audit against ENAG. — [Chequeado](https://chequeado.com/hilando-fino/a-falta-de-datos-de-inflacion/); [Duvar English](https://www.duvarenglish.com/turkish-govt-statistics-office-tuik-files-lawsuit-against-enagroup-over-alternative-inflation-data-news-57435)
- **Privacy-first design as a feature**: Pocket Index (no accounts, no analytics, on-device OCR) and thrust ("privacy-first", manual/CSV). — [Pocket Index](https://github.com/Siyan-Dimitrov/personal-inflation-tracker); [thrust](https://thrust.finance/learn/personal-inflation-rate/)
- **Data-representativeness caution** for bank or card data. — [arXiv 2607.08759](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.08759)
- **What sticks commercially**: PriceStats built 15 years of methodology, sold to institutions and central banks, and was acquired by State Street in 2025. Truflation pivoted into a token-based data network (TRUF.NETWORK) with DeFi integrations. — [Financial Investigator](https://www.financialinvestigator.nl/en/nieuws-detailpagina/2025/11/10/state-street-acquires-pricestats-global-leader-in-inflation-analytics); [Truflation](https://blog.truflation.com/truflation-truf-network-and-the-truf-token-the-next-chapter/)

### Inferences
- **Why consumer personal-inflation tools struggle** (synthesized from the evidence above):
  - (1) User effort: manual category entry is the norm, and connecting a bank raises the privacy bar.
  - (2) Data granularity: bank transactions give merchant and amount but not items or prices, so mapping to roughly 299 INPC genéricos is guesswork beyond coarse categories. A supermarket ticket spans dozens of genéricos.
  - (3) Novelty decay: you learn your number once, and it moves only slowly month to month when official inflation is ~3%.
  - (4) A weak "so what": without an action attached (a raise ask, a budget cut, an investment benchmark), the number is trivia.
  - (5) Conceptual confusion: many tools measure spending growth, not price change.
- **What could make the user's project stick**, based on what worked elsewhere:
  - Attach the number to a decision. Truflation's "salary cut" and raise framing and the HN interest in inflation-matching raises point this way, as does the user's own "investment benchmark" idea (real return vs. personal inflation, CETES/UDIBONOS comparisons).
  - Automate monthly updates around INEGI's release calendar.
  - Be transparent and reproducible: open Laspeyres code and reconciliation to the headline INPC.
  - Keep it local-first for privacy.
  - Treat reweighting as "composition inflation" and disclose that prices actually paid (brand and store choice) aren't captured unless receipts or SKU data are added.
- In Mexico, using official INEGI genérico indices avoids the political risk alternative price indices faced in Argentina and Turkey, and frames the tool as *complementing* rather than *contradicting* INEGI.

### Gaps
- No retention or engagement data for any personal-inflation feature (DAU, repeat use) was found anywhere. Statements about novelty decay are inference, not measured.
- No user-research or survey data on Mexicans' interest in personal-inflation tools was found.
- Revenue figures for Truflation and PriceStats aren't public. The Dealroom revenue-model note is an estimate.
